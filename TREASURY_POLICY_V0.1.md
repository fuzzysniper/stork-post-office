# STORK POST OFFICE — Treasury Policy v0.1

Status: ACTIVE POLICY / CONSERVATIVE GENESIS PHASE

Treasury address: `6VGA2f3Ya9SVPrbcpBpjrgBRMqkQyEgZbmpx5mFNFTyT`

## Purpose
The Post Office treasury exists to build durable delivery infrastructure around STORK. It is not a chart-support wallet and must not be used to manufacture volume, holder count, or apparent organic demand.

## Accounting buckets

### 1. Reserve Principal
Assets contributed directly to the Post Office, including creator contributions such as externally earned STONK.

Rules:
- Reserve Principal is held by default.
- No STORK buybacks from Reserve Principal during genesis phase.
- No route deployment until a route clears the Route Engine activation rule and a deployment limit is documented.
- Contributions remain auditable on-chain.

### 2. Route Principal
Reserve capital explicitly allocated to a declassified route after approval.

Rules:
- Every allocation is labeled as treasury activity.
- Position size, entry transaction, route, and exit/liquidity constraints are recorded.
- Route Principal is tracked separately from rewards generated by that position.

### 3. Route Revenue
Rewards generated by approved treasury route positions.

Rules:
- Revenue is accounted separately from principal.
- Revenue may later be split among reserve growth, route compounding, and a disclosed STORK ecosystem benefit mechanism.
- No revenue split is active until explicitly published.

## Genesis-phase STORK benefit
No automatic buyback, burn, or bonus distribution is active in v0.1.

The intended future STORK Benefit bucket may include transparent open-market STORK acquisition sent to a disclosed long-duration reserve/lock, or additional holder deliveries, but neither mechanism is promised or active today.

## Prohibited activity
- circular trading
- wash trading
- fake / controlled wallets presented as organic holders
- undisclosed treasury buys presented as organic demand
- spending Reserve Principal solely to support price
- representing treasury assets as redeemable backing unless an explicit legal/on-chain redemption right is created
- advertising guaranteed yield, APR, or profit from treasury operations
